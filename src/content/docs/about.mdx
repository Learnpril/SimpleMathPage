Mom's Basement University is a free, unofficial math resource for adults who want to learn
or relearn mathematics properly, without a classroom, a deadline, or anyone watching. It is
not an accredited school and grants no credit or diplomas.

## Who this is for

People who never got a fair shot at math the first time, and people who did fine but
forgot most of it. There is no placement test and no assumed starting point. The
Foundations group genuinely begins at counting.

It is also for people who already know some math and want a specific tool: how
quaternions work, why a shader needs a dot product, what backpropagation computes.
That is what the Applied track is for.

## How it is organised

**[Core Mathematics](/core-mathematics/)** is the rigorous track: eighteen subjects in
learning order, from arithmetic through topology. Every lesson has worked examples and
a quiz, and the upper-division subjects prove their results rather than asserting them.

**[Applied Mathematics](/applied-mathematics/)** is the practical track: seven modules
aimed at a craft - games, 3D, shaders, AI and more. Two are finished, the 2D and 3D game
development modules, at 43 sections between them; the rest are in development. Applied is
structured as a guide and reference rather than a lesson sequence. There are no quizzes,
and the emphasis is on showing how the math works in context rather than building it from
axioms.

Core is a sequence. Applied is a set of entry points that lean on Core and link back
to it wherever they need something proved.

## How the lessons are built

Every lesson follows the same shape: what you will learn, the concept, worked examples,
where it shows up in the real world, and a quiz. Figures are drawn from computed values
rather than by hand, so a diagram cannot quietly disagree with the text beside it.

Progress is tracked per lesson. It works without an account using local storage, and
syncs across devices if you sign in.

## What it costs

Nothing to read. Every lesson on this site is free and none of it sits behind a login or
a paywall.

The site may show advertising to help cover hosting and keep it online. There is no
subscription and no email capture. Signing in is optional: it exists so your progress
follows you between devices, and that is the only thing it does. Progress tracking works
perfectly well without an account, using your browser's local storage.

## What this is not

Mom's Basement University is an **unofficial** free math resource. It is not an
accredited school, it is not affiliated with any university, and it does not grant
credit, diplomas, certificates or transcripts. The name is a joke about where a lot of us
do our best thinking.

It is written for **adults** relearning mathematics — people who never got a fair shot at
it, and people who did fine and forgot most of it. It is not a children's education
service and is not directed at children under 13.
