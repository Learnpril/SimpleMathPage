## What You'll Learn

This review covers every major topic from Pre-Algebra in one place — variables, expressions, equations, inequalities, exponents, proportions, absolute value, and graphing. Each section has a quick refresher and a worked example so you can brush up before moving on.

## Variables & Expressions

A **variable** is a letter that stands for an unknown number. An **expression** combines variables, numbers, and operations (but no equals sign).

To **evaluate** an expression, substitute the given values and follow order of operations.

Evaluate $3x + 2y - 5$ when $x = 4$ and $y = 3$

$$
\begin{aligned}
3(4) + 2(3) - 5 &\\
= 12 + 6 - 5 &\\
= 13
\end{aligned}
$$

## Signed Numbers

Same signs → add and keep the sign. Different signs → subtract and keep the sign of the larger absolute value.

For multiplication/division: same signs → positive, different signs → negative.

$$
\begin{aligned}
{-}8 + 5 &= {-}3 \\
({-}6) \times ({-}4) &= 24 \\
15 \div ({-}3) &= {-}5
\end{aligned}
$$

## Order of Operations

PEMDAS: Parentheses → Exponents → Multiply/Divide (left to right) → Add/Subtract (left to right).

Evaluate $4 + 3(2x - 1)^2$ when $x = 2$

$$
\begin{aligned}
4 + 3(2(2) - 1)^2 &\\
= 4 + 3(4 - 1)^2 &\\
= 4 + 3(3)^2 &\\
= 4 + 3(9) &\\
= 4 + 27 = 31
\end{aligned}
$$

## Combining Like Terms & Distributive Property

**Like terms** have the same variable and exponent. Combine by adding/subtracting coefficients.

The **distributive property**: _a_(_b_ + _c_) = _ab_ + _ac_.

Simplify $2(3x + 4) - 5x + 1$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(3x + 4) - 5x + 1 &\\
= 6x + 8 - 5x + 1 &\\
= x + 9
\end{aligned}
$$

## Solving Equations

**One-step**: Do the inverse operation on both sides.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x + 12 &= 20 \\
x &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

**Two-step**: Undo add/subtract first, then multiply/divide.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x - 7 &= 14 \\
3x &= 21 \\
x &= 7
\end{aligned}
$$

**Variables on both sides**: Move all variable terms to one side first.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5x + 3 &= 2x + 18 \\
3x + 3 &= 18 \\
3x &= 15 \\
x &= 5
\end{aligned}
$$

Check: 5(5) + 3 = 28 and 2(5) + 18 = 28 ✓

## Inequalities

Solve like equations, but **flip the sign** when multiplying/dividing by a negative.

$$
\begin{aligned}
{-}2x + 6 &> 12 \\
{-}2x &> 6 \\
x &< {-}3 \quad \text{(flip: divided by }{-}2\text{)}
\end{aligned}
$$

Graph: open circle at −3, shade left.

## Exponents & Powers

$a^n$ means multiply _a_ by itself _n_ times. Key rules:

- $a^0 = 1$
- $a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$
- $a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2^5 &= 32 \\
4^{-2} &= \frac{1}{16} \\
3^2 \times 3^3 &= 3^5 = 243
\end{aligned}
$$

## Proportions & Ratios

A proportion is two equal ratios: _a_/_b_ = _c_/_d_. Cross-multiply to solve.

A recipe uses 3 cups of flour for 18 muffins. How much for 30 muffins?

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{3}{18} &= \frac{x}{30} \\
3 \times 30 &= 18x \\
90 &= 18x \\
x &= 5 \text{ cups}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Absolute Value

$|x|$ is the distance from 0 — always positive or zero. Equations split into two cases.

Solve $|2x - 1| = 7$

**Case 1:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x - 1 &= 7 \\
2x &= 8 \\
x &= 4
\end{aligned}
$$

**Case 2:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x - 1 &= {-}7 \\
2x &= {-}6 \\
x &= {-}3
\end{aligned}
$$

Check: |2(4) − 1| = |7| = 7 ✓ and |2(−3) − 1| = |−7| = 7 ✓

## Word Problems

Translate words into equations, solve, and check.

You earn 18 dollars per hour and need at least 500 dollars for rent. How many hours minimum?

$$
\begin{aligned}
18h &\geq 500 \\
h &\geq 27.8
\end{aligned}
$$

You need at least 28 hours.

## Basic Graphing

Points are (_x_, _y_). Lines in slope-intercept form: _y_ = _mx_ + _b_.

Graph $y = 2x - 1$

| $x$  | $y$  |
| ---- | ---- |
| $0$  | $-1$ |
| $1$  | $1$  |
| $2$  | $3$  |
| $-1$ | $-3$ |

## Mixed Practice

Try these on your own before checking the quiz:

1. Evaluate $4(x - 3) + 2x$ when $x = 5$ → $4(2) + 10 = 18$
2. Solve $6x + 1 = 3x + 16$ → $x = 5$
3. Solve $-3y \leq 21$ → $y \geq -7$
4. Simplify $5^3 \times 5^{-1}$ → $5^2 = 25$
5. Solve $\frac{4}{7} = \frac{x}{21}$ → $x = 12$
6. Solve $|x - 4| = 9$ → $x = 13$ or $x = -5$
