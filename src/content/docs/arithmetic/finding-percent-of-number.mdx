## What You'll Learn

In this lesson you'll learn two easy ways to find a percent of a number: using decimals or fractions.

## The Concept

To find **a percent of a number**, convert the percent to a decimal (or fraction) and multiply.

Methods:

1. Decimal way: Percent as decimal × number.
   $20\%$ of $150 = 0.20 \times 150 = 30$

2. Fraction way: Percent as fraction × number.
   $20\% = \frac{1}{5} → \frac{1}{5} \times 150 = 30$

Same result. Use whichever is easier mentally.

For 10%, 5%, 1%: Move decimal left (10% of 150 = 15), halve for 5%, etc.

## Worked Examples

Find 35% of 240 dollars.

1. $35\% = 0.35$
2. $0.35 \times 240 = 84$ dollars

Alternative: $35\% = \frac{35}{100} = \frac{7}{20}$

$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{7}{20} \times 240 &= \frac{7 \times 240}{20} \\[1em]
&= \frac{1680}{20} \\[1em]
&= 84 \text{ dollars}
\end{aligned}
$

## Real-World Applications

Finding a percent of a number calculates tips (15% of 60 dollars bill = 9 dollars), discounts (25% of 80 dollars item = 20 dollars off), tax (7% of 300 dollars purchase = 21 dollars tax), or commission (5% of 2000 dollars sales = 100 dollars). It's a daily skill for money decisions.

:::note[You've Got This]
Convert the percent to a decimal (move two places left) and multiply — that's the fastest way for most problems. Practice with tips or sales tax you see every day. You'll get quick and accurate in no time.
:::
