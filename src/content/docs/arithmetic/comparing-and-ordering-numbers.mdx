## What You'll Learn

In this lesson you'll learn how to compare two or more numbers and put them in order from least to greatest or greatest to least.

## The Concept

**Comparing numbers** uses symbols:

- &gt; means greater than (bigger)
- &lt; means less than (smaller)
- = means equal

To compare: Line up digits by place value (right-align), compare from left to right.

Example: 456 and 432

- Hundreds: 4 = 4
- Tens: 5 &gt; 3 → 456 &gt; 432

If same up to a point: 567 and 569 → 567 &lt; 569 (7 &lt; 9)

**Ordering**: List from smallest to largest (least to greatest) or largest to smallest.

Example: Order 23, 18, 45, 9
Least to greatest: 9, 18, 23, 45

## Worked Example

Compare 378 and 392.

1. Hundreds: 3 = 3
2. Tens: 7 &lt; 9 → 378 &lt; 392

Order 512, 489, 500, 475:

1. Hundreds: 4 (489, 475), 5 (512, 500)
2. Among 5xx: 500 &lt; 512
3. Among 4xx: 475 &lt; 489
4. Least to greatest: 475, 489, 500, 512

## Real-World Application

Comparing and ordering numbers helps daily:

- Prices: Which is cheaper — 12.99 dollars or 13.49 dollars? (12.99 &lt; 13.49)
- Temperatures: Today 68°F, yesterday 72°F — yesterday was warmer (72 &gt; 68)
- Scores: Your test 85, friend's 92 — friend scored higher (92 &gt; 85)
- Addresses: House numbers in order on a street (124, 126, 128...)

This skill helps you choose, compare, or organize things quickly.

:::note[You've Got This]
Comparing numbers is like lining up people by height — look at the biggest place first. If tied, look at the next place. Practice with prices, temperatures, or scores you see every day — you'll spot which is bigger or smaller instantly.
:::
