## What You'll Learn

In this lesson you'll learn simple rules to convert percentages to decimals and fractions, and back again.

## The Concept

Conversions are straightforward:

- Percent to decimal: Move decimal point 2 places left (divide by 100).
  $45\% → 0.45$

- Percent to fraction: Put number over 100, simplify.
  $45\% → \frac{45}{100} = \frac{9}{20}$

- Decimal to percent: Move decimal 2 places right (multiply by 100).
  $0.75 → 75\%$

- Fraction to percent: Divide numerator by denominator, then convert decimal to percent.
  $\frac{3}{4} = 0.75 = 75\%$

- Fraction to decimal: Divide numerator by denominator.

## Worked Examples

Convert each:

1. $20\% → $ decimal $0.20 → $ fraction $\frac{20}{100} = \frac{1}{5}$
2. $0.625 → $ percent $62.5\% → $ fraction $\frac{625}{1000} = \frac{5}{8}$
3. $\frac{2}{5} → $ decimal $0.4 → $ percent $40\%$

## Real-World Applications

Conversions help compare offers: 1/4 off = 0.25 = 25% discount. Or tax: 8% = 0.08, so $0.08 \times 200$ dollars = 16 dollars tax. Or interest: 6.5% = 0.065. Knowing how to switch makes shopping, budgeting, and finance clearer.

:::note[You've Got This]
The big trick is moving the decimal two places for percent ↔ decimal. For fractions, just divide or simplify. Practice converting tips or discounts you see daily — it will stick fast.
:::
