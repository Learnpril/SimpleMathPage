## What You'll Learn

In this lesson you'll learn how to divide decimals by converting the divisor to a whole number, then using long division to find the answer.

## The Concept

**Dividing decimals** uses one key trick: move the decimal point in the divisor to make it a whole number, then move the decimal in the dividend the same number of places.

Example: $6.5 \div 0.5$

1. Move decimal 1 place right in both: $65 \div 5$
2. Divide:

$$
\begin{array}{r|rr}
  & 1 & 3 \\
  \hline
  5 & 6 & 5 \\
  & {-}5 & \\
  \hline
  & 1 & 5 \\
  & {-}1 & 5 \\
  \hline
  & & 0
\end{array}
$$

Result: $13$

Another: $4.56 \div 0.12$

1. Move decimal 2 places right in both: $456 \div 12$
2. Divide:

$$
\begin{array}{r|rrr}
  & & 3 & 8 \\
  \hline
  12 & 4 & 5 & 6 \\
  & {-}3 & 6 & \\
  \hline
  & & 9 & 6 \\
  & & {-}9 & 6 \\
  \hline
  & & & 0
\end{array}
$$

Result: $38$

When dividing a decimal by a whole number, just divide normally and bring the decimal straight up into the quotient.

Example: $9.36 \div 4$

$$
\begin{array}{r|rrrr}
  & 2 & . & 3 & 4 \\
  \hline
  4 & 9 & . & 3 & 6 \\
  & {-}8 & & & \\
  \hline
  & 1 & & 3 & \\
  & {-}1 & & 2 & \\
  \hline
  & & & 1 & 6 \\
  & & & {-}1 & 6 \\
  \hline
  & & & & 0
\end{array}
$$

Result: $2.34$

## Worked Example

Divide $7.2 \div 0.6$.

1. Move decimal 1 place right in both: $72 \div 6$
2. Long division:

$$
\begin{array}{r|rr}
  & 1 & 2 \\
  \hline
  6 & 7 & 2 \\
  & {-}6 & \\
  \hline
  & 1 & 2 \\
  & {-}1 & 2 \\
  \hline
  & & 0
\end{array}
$$

Result: $12$

Divide $15.75 \div 2.5$.

1. Move decimal 1 place right in both: $157.5 \div 25$
2. Long division:

$$
\begin{array}{r|rrrrr}
  & & & 6 & . & 3 \\
  \hline
  25 & 1 & 5 & 7 & . & 5 \\
  & {-}1 & 5 & 0 & & \\
  \hline
  & & & 7 & & 5 \\
  & & & {-}7 & & 5 \\
  \hline
  & & & & & 0
\end{array}
$$

Result: $6.3$

Divide $0.648 \div 0.08$.

1. Move decimal 2 places right in both: $64.8 \div 8$
2. Long division:

$$
\begin{array}{r|rrrr}
  & 8 & . & 1 & \\
  \hline
  8 & 6 & 4 & . & 8 \\
  & {-}6 & 4 & & \\
  \hline
  & & 0 & & 8 \\
  & & & {-} & 8 \\
  \hline
  & & & & 0
\end{array}
$$

Result: $8.1$

## Real-World Application

Dividing decimals helps split costs (a 47.50 dollar dinner split 5 ways = 9.50 dollars each), calculate unit prices (3.78 dollars for 1.5 pounds = 2.52 dollars per pound), or figure out mileage (325.5 miles on 12.5 gallons = 26.04 miles per gallon). Any time you need a rate or per-unit cost, you're dividing decimals.

:::note[You've Got This]
The trick is making the divisor a whole number first — shift both decimals the same way and it's just regular long division from there. Practice with prices and measurements, and always estimate first to check your answer makes sense.
:::
