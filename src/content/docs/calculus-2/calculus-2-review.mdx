## Key Formulas

### Integration Techniques

Integration by parts

$$
\int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du
$$

Trig substitution patterns

$$
\sqrt{a^2 - x^2} \to x = a\sin\theta \qquad \sqrt{a^2 + x^2} \to x = a\tan\theta \qquad \sqrt{x^2 - a^2} \to x = a\sec\theta
$$

Improper integrals: replace the problematic bound with a variable and take the limit

$$
\int_a^{\infty} f(x)\,dx = \lim_{b \to \infty} \int_a^b f(x)\,dx
$$

### Applications of Integration

Area between curves

$$
A = \int_a^b \left[f(x) - g(x)\right]dx \quad \text{where } f(x) \geq g(x)
$$

Volumes of revolution

$$
V_{\text{disk}} = \pi\int_a^b [R(x)]^2\,dx \qquad V_{\text{washer}} = \pi\int_a^b \left[R_{\text{outer}}^2 - R_{\text{inner}}^2\right]dx
$$

$$
V_{\text{shell}} = 2\pi\int_a^b x\,h(x)\,dx
$$

Arc length

$$
L = \int_a^b \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2}\,dx
$$

Work by a variable force

$$
W = \int_a^b F(x)\,dx
$$

Average value

$$
f_{\text{avg}} = \frac{1}{b - a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx
$$

### Differential Equations

Separable: separate variables and integrate both sides

$$
\frac{dy}{dx} = f(x)\,g(y) \quad \Rightarrow \quad \int \frac{1}{g(y)}\,dy = \int f(x)\,dx
$$

Linear first-order: multiply by the integrating factor

$$
\frac{dy}{dx} + P(x)\,y = Q(x) \qquad \mu(x) = e^{\int P(x)\,dx}
$$

### Series Formulas

Geometric series

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} ar^n = \frac{a}{1 - r} \quad \text{when } |r| \lt 1
$$

p-series

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^p} \quad \text{converges if } p \gt 1, \quad \text{diverges if } p \leq 1
$$

Ratio test

$$
L = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right| \quad \text{converges if } L \lt 1, \quad \text{diverges if } L \gt 1
$$

Alternating series test: converges if terms decrease and approach zero

Power series centered at a

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n(x - a)^n \quad \text{converges when } |x - a| \lt R
$$

Taylor series centered at a

$$
f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x - a)^n
$$

Lagrange remainder (error bound)

$$
R_n(x) = \frac{f^{(n+1)}(c)}{(n+1)!}(x - a)^{n+1}
$$

Common Maclaurin series

$$
e^x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!} \qquad \sin x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!} \qquad \cos x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{x^{2n}}{(2n)!}
$$

$$
\frac{1}{1-x} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} x^n \quad \text{for } |x| \lt 1
$$

## Mixed Worked Examples

**1. Integration by parts**

$$
\int xe^x\,dx
$$

$$
u = x, \quad dv = e^x\,dx, \quad du = dx, \quad v = e^x
$$

$$
\int xe^x\,dx = xe^x - \int e^x\,dx = xe^x - e^x + C = e^x(x - 1) + C
$$

**2. Volume by shells**

Region between y = x and y = x² from x = 0 to x = 1, rotated around the y-axis.

$$
V = 2\pi\int_0^1 x(x - x^2)\,dx = 2\pi\int_0^1 (x^2 - x^3)\,dx
$$

$$
= 2\pi\left[\frac{x^3}{3} - \frac{x^4}{4}\right]_0^1 = 2\pi\left(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{4}\right) = 2\pi\cdot\frac{1}{12} = \frac{\pi}{6}
$$

**3. Separable differential equation**

Solve dy/dx = 2xy.

$$
\frac{1}{y}\,dy = 2x\,dx \quad \Rightarrow \quad \ln|y| = x^2 + C \quad \Rightarrow \quad y = Ae^{x^2}
$$

**4. Convergence test (ratio test)**

Does the series converge?

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!}
$$

$$
L = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{|x|^{n+1}/(n+1)!}{|x|^n/n!} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{|x|}{n+1} = 0 \lt 1
$$

Converges for all x. This is the series for e to the x.
