There is no form, no ticket number and no chatbot. It is a mailbox.

## What gets a fast reply

**A mistake in the math.** This is the most useful message you can send and it goes to the
front of the queue. Tell us the lesson and roughly where — "the third worked example" is
plenty. If a figure disagrees with the text beside it, that is a real bug and we want it.

**A broken page.** Something not loading, a diagram that never appears, a quiz that will
not submit. Mentioning your browser and whether you are on a phone helps a lot.

**Account or data deletion.** Email from the address you registered with and say you want
the account gone. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for what that removes.

## What gets a slower reply

**Requests to reuse content.** Usually you do not need to ask. The lessons are openly
licensed and attribution is enough — see the [License page](/license/) for the credit line
and what it covers. Write if your situation is not covered there.

**Suggestions for new subjects or lessons.** Read and appreciated, but the queue is long
and this is not a full-time operation.

**Institutional enquiries.** Schools, libraries, adult education and other programs: read,
and answered when there is time. If you only wanted permission to teach from the lessons,
the license already gives it to you and you can start without waiting for a reply.

## What will not get a reply

**Homework.** We cannot do your assignment, sit your exam, or check your answers one by
one. The lessons and their worked examples are the help on offer, and they are free.

**Advertising and sponsorship enquiries.** The site carries no advertising and no
sponsorships, and no ad space is for sale.

**Anything to do with paying for this.** This site is not taking payments. There is nothing
to buy, no subscription, no tip jar and no fundraising campaign, so there is nothing to
discuss and no invoice to chase.

:::note[Please do not send personal information]
Do not email medical, financial or identity documents. There is no reason this site would
ever need them, and nobody here will ask you for them.

**Mom's Basement University will never ask you for money or for a password.** It is not
taking payments at all. If a message claiming to be from this site asks you for either, it
is not us — no matter what the address looks like.
:::

## Response time

Usually a few days. Sometimes longer. This is a free site maintained around a job, so
patience is genuinely appreciated — and a clear subject line helps more than a follow-up.
