## What is Differential Equations?

Differential equations are equations that relate a function to its own rate of change. In plain terms, they let us mathematically describe how things evolve over time, whether it's population growth, the swinging of a bridge, the spread of heat, or the motion of planets.

Instead of asking "what is the value right now?", we ask "how is the value changing, and what will it become?"

## Where It Came From

People have been wrestling with rates of change since Newton and Leibniz invented calculus. Differential equations exploded in importance during the 1700s and 1800s when scientists needed tools to model the physical world. Today they're the backbone of modern engineering, physics, biology, economics, and even machine learning.

## Why It Matters

Almost everything that changes can be modeled with differential equations. Engineers use them to design stable bridges and safe airplanes. Biologists model predator-prey relationships. Climate scientists simulate global temperatures. Game developers use them for realistic physics. If you want to understand how the real world actually works, this is the language.
