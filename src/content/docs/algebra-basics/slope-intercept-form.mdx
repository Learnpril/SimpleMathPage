## What You'll Learn

Slope-intercept form is $y = mx + b$, where $m$ is the slope and $b$ is the y-intercept. This lesson shows how to read a line's equation, graph it from those two numbers, and write the equation of a line you are given.

## The Concept

**Slope-intercept form** is the equation y = mx + b, where:

- m = slope (rate of change: how much y changes per unit of x)
- b = y-intercept (the value of y when x = 0; where the line crosses the y-axis)

This form makes graphing easy: start at y-intercept b, then use slope m to move up/down and right/left.

Steps to graph y = mx + b:

1. Plot the y-intercept point (0, b)
2. From that point, use slope m = rise/run
   - Positive m: up and right
   - Negative m: down and right
   - Fraction m = p/q: up p, right q (or down if negative)
3. Plot a second point, then draw a straight line through both

Example: y = 2x + 3

- y-intercept b = 3 → point (0, 3)
- Slope m = 2 = 2/1 → from (0, 3): up 2, right 1 → point (1, 5)
- Draw line through (0, 3) and (1, 5)

## Worked Example

Graph y = −3x + 6.

1. y-intercept b = 6 → point (0, 6)
2. Slope m = −3 = −3/1 → from (0, 6): down 3, right 1 → point (1, 3)
3. Draw line through (0, 6) and (1, 3) (continues down-right)

Another: y = (1/2)x − 4

1. y-intercept b = −4 → point (0, −4)
2. Slope m = 1/2 → from (0, −4): up 1, right 2 → point (2, −3)
3. Draw line through (0, −4) and (2, −3)

## Real-World Application

Slope-intercept form models linear relationships:

- Pay: y = 18x + 50 (18 dollars per hour + 50-dollar bonus; y = total pay, x = hours)
- Cost: y = 3.50x + 20 (3.50 dollars per gallon gas + 20-dollar delivery fee)
- Savings: y = 0.05x + 200 (5% interest per year + 200 dollars initial; y = amount, x = years)
- Distance: y = 65x (65 mph driving; y = miles, x = hours)

Slope = rate of change (pay rate, cost per unit, interest rate)

y-intercept = starting amount (bonus, fee, initial deposit)

This form helps predict totals, compare plans, or plan budgets.

:::note[You've Got This]
Slope-intercept form is the easiest way to see a line's story: b is the starting point, m is the speed/direction of change. Start at (0, b), follow the slope (rise/run), draw the line. Practice with real rates (pay, gas, savings) and you'll quickly understand what the equation is telling you.
:::
